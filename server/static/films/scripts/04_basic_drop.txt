2. And no two RED squares are worth the same.
3. Hover one. The card prices it for you.
4. TRACE. Purity 30 — and a 0.75 penalty on top. Twenty-three.
5. That is one hour of a 21-hour night, and one slot of a six-slot hold, for twenty-three points.
6. VEIN. 107 at 1.0 — what you see is what you bank.
7. Ordinary. But there is a lot of vein, and seven of these is a jackpot.
8. MASS. 203 at 1.5 — three hundred and five.
9. The workhorse. Two good MASS squares are worth about one jackpot, and they are far easier to find.
10. PURE. 255 at 3.0. Seven hundred and sixty-five.
11. One square. One hour. Thirty-three TRACE squares.
12. A pure changes the pace of a game — which is why nobody lets you keep one quietly.
13. So: never walk for trace. Walk a run of vein. Fight for mass. And take a pure the hour you can reach it.
14. DROP puts a harvester on the surface — and only where you can see RIGHT NOW.
15. Every square you enter is harvested automatically. Keep clicking.
16. Six squares — a landing and five steps. That is the whole hold, and the last one is the 765.
17. Then LIFT, always. Ore on the surface at dawn is not ore.
18. The landing harvests the square it lands on — 237, mass.
19. Up the seam, one square an hour.
20. And the last one is the pure. 765.
21. Lifted on hour seven with a full hold — 2188 off one seam.
22. Now look at what you left behind.
23. The same square. An hour ago it was 765.
24. Every RED square you harvest turns GREEN. Green is worth MINUS one hundred.
25. And harvesting is automatic — anything that walks in here has no choice about picking it up.
26. So you did not just bank 2188. You laid six of those across the best seam on the board.
27. Including for yourself. Never walk back over your own trail.
