[Part 1 — Rush]
1. The other House has an eye on their jackpot.
2. One EMP launch is a salvo of three.
3. Each missile is a radius-2 diamond. Overlap them and you get a wall, not three puddles.
4. Eight hours of cloud over their seam. Now go and take it.
5. Land clear of the wall, then walk straight in. Five squares, ending on their 255.
6. Three missiles, one wall.
7. You land one square outside it.
8. First step in — and the cloud denies the harvest.
9. Disabled. Inside a live cloud a hull does not act at all.
10. Lifted on hour nine with an empty hold.
11. Nothing. Not a reduced haul — nothing.
12. You spent the EMP, the probe and the whole night walking through your own weapon.

[Part 2 — Wait]
13. Again. Same salvo, same hull, same five squares.
14. One EMP launch is a salvo of three.
15. Each missile is a radius-2 diamond. Overlap them and you get a wall, not three puddles.
16. The cloud is live from hour one to hour EIGHT. You know that because you set it.
17. So spend the hours first. Five of them, on nothing.
18. Five hours of a 21-hour night, burned on purpose.
19. Now land, and walk in behind the clock.
20. Three missiles, one wall.
21. Nothing happening, deliberately.
22. Landing outside the wall on the hour it expires.
23. Hour nine. The cloud is gone, and you step in.
24. Harvesting all the way down the row.
25. And the last square is their jackpot. 765.
26. 1078 banked, off a seam they lit and never reached.
27. They could read that timer too. The difference is you were already standing next to it when it ran out.
28. EMP does not take the red. It takes the CLOCK — and the clock is only worth having if you wait for it.
