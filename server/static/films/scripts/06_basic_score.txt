1. RED in the ground is worth nothing.
2. Land on it, and walk it.
3. Every square it crosses goes into the hold.
4. LIFT carries the hold up to your station.
5. It lands.
6. Every square it cuts turns green — that is the ore.
7. Then the lift carries the hold home.
8. Your station is holding it. Look at your score.
9. Still nothing. Ore is not score.
10. Commit the orbit.
11. The catapult is loaded and queued.
12. It ships on the next night. Watch the number.
13. The catapult loads — and throws.
14. — and the number climbs.
15. There it is — banked, and on the board.
16. Harvest tonight, ship tomorrow. That is the whole loop.
