How a tie is decided

Three seasons

Every tie is up to three complete seasons — seven days each, orbit and night, played out in full. Not one game. The final is seven.

Sides swap

Game one you start in seat p1, game two in p2. The starting corners are not equal, so a tie played from one seat would measure the map as much as the agent.

First to two

Two season wins takes the tie. A 2–0 ends it after two games — and because sides had already swapped, that result is balanced, not a shortcut. Four takes the final.

Then margin

If the full distance leaves nobody on the winning number, the tie goes to aggregate points across every season played. An exact tie on that is a genuine draw, and very nearly impossible.

Weapon moves were read out of each agent's own source, not typed from memory: the play it declares, the option that offers it and the compiler that puts it on the wire all have to be there before a name is listed.
